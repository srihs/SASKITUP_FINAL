Proper Care for Longevity
Washing
  • Machine wash cold (30°C max)
  • Turn inside out before washing
  • Use mild detergent only
  • Wash with similar colors
  • Do not use bleach or fabric softener
Drying
  • Tumble dry on low heat
  • Remove promptly to prevent wrinkles
  • Air dry for best results
  • Do not wring or twist when wet
Ironing
  • Iron on medium heat (150°C)
  • Iron inside out to protect crest
  • Use pressing cloth over embroidery
  • Iron collar and cuffs carefully
Storage
  • Hang on padded hangers
  • Store in dry, ventilated area
  • Avoid direct sunlight storage
  • Keep embroidery protected
Important: Following these care instructions will ensure your school polo maintains its appearance and quality throughout the school year. Improper care may void warranty coverage.
